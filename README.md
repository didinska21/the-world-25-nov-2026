# THE WORLD — Wednesday 25 November 2026

One day of world news, one picture: a full newspaper front page for Wednesday
25 November 2026, drawn entirely in code (Python + PIL) so every headline
stays perfectly legible.

**30 date-checked stories · 8 regions — every one happened or was first
reported on this Wednesday.**

## The leads

- Shenzhou-24 lifts off with the first Pakistani in orbit, bound for
  Tiangong station (East Asia)
- Russian strike kills two in Odesa; homes and cars hit (Europe)
- Rescuers hunt for dozens trapped 100 m deep in a flooded Java gold mine
  (Southeast Asia)
- Iowa counts down to the 9 AM blast of 68 barrels of unstable lead azide
  (North America)
- Australia formally apologises to LGBTQ veterans: "Today, it says sorry"
  (Oceania)
- A judge hears Hasan's bid to halt the Dec 3 firing-squad execution, the
  first US military execution in 65 years (North America)

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies the bundled `entry.png`
to the output directory, byte for byte, on every run.

## How it was made

The front page is drawn, not photographed: six lead panels with hand-drawn
scenes (Shenzhou-24 on the pad, burning Odesa block, flooded mine rescue,
Iowa blast countdown, Parliament House with the apology banner, courtroom
scales), 24 brief cards, an eight-region colour key, a UTC timeline strip,
a world pin map, and a date footer. Every story was checked against a report
dated Wednesday 25 November 2026 before it made the page — a story counts
only if it happened or was first reported on that Wednesday; Tuesday's news,
however widely republished on Wednesday, was left out. Items still to come
that day (the Iowa blast, US inflation data, the Ofgem cap, the Hasan
hearing) are labelled as due or scheduled, never as already happened.

Story count by region: North America 4, Europe 5, Middle East 3, Africa 2,
South Asia 2, East Asia 6, Southeast Asia 4, Oceania 4 — 30 stories in
total across 8 regions.
